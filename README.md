# Drug-Performance-Analytics-Patient-Sentiment-Analysis-Dashboard
Aanalyze drug effectiveness, customer satisfaction, and market trends

Key Insights:

1. Drug Performance Analysis

Effectiveness Trends: Some drug categories, such as Neurological & Psychiatric, require more research and development as they have lower effectiveness ratings.
Customer Satisfaction: One of the highest-selling drugs has only an average rating, while another high-seller has a very poor score, signaling a potential concern for the company.
Ease of Use vs. Effectiveness: Ease of use has a stronger correlation with customer satisfaction than effectiveness.

2. Comparative Drug Insights

Top Drugs for Each Condition: Some conditions have no highly effective drugs, presenting an opportunity for R&D investment.
On-Label vs. Off-Label Use: On-label drugs are more effective across all categories. Off-label drugs have a wider variability, particularly in cardiovascular & gastrointestinal categories, requiring stricter monitoring. Neurological & psychiatric drugs have similar effectiveness for both on-label and off-label use, suggesting potential for expanded applications.
RX vs. OTC Satisfaction: Over-the-counter (OTC) drugs tend to receive higher satisfaction ratings compared to prescription (RX) drugs.

3. Market Insights

Most Reviewed Drugs: Heavily reviewed drugs do not necessarily have the best ratings—highlighting either strong market trust or high user dissatisfaction.
Low Satisfaction Conditions: Certain conditions have low satisfaction across multiple drugs, suggesting the need for alternative treatments.
Effectiveness of Drug Types: OTC drugs tend to be more positively reviewed, potentially indicating that easier access leads to better customer perception.

4. Business Strategy & Growth

Improving Drug Formulations: Pharmaceutical companies should focus on conditions where drugs consistently receive low ratings.
Marketing & Promotions: Highly reviewed, highly rated drugs should be marketed more aggressively. Highly reviewed, poorly rated drugs should be analyzed for potential improvements or rebranding.
Regulatory & Clinical Trials: Off-label drugs in high-variability categories require closer monitoring and possible regulatory adjustments. Encouraging clinical trials for promising off-label drugs can expand their use cases.
